What We Build

## Our Core Services

From ground-breaking to final inspection — we manage every phase with precision, transparency, and accountability.

### Residential Construction

New homes from single-room additions to full double-storey houses. NHBRC-compliant builds across all Pretoria suburbs — Soshanguve, Mabopane, Pretoria North and beyond.

### Commercial Development

Offices, retail spaces, warehouses, and mixed-use developments delivered on schedule. Full project documentation and professional oversight from inception to handover.

### Renovations & Extensions

Kitchen and bathroom remodels, extensions, internal alterations, and full-property makeovers — handled efficiently with minimal disruption to your household.

### Project Management

End-to-end coordination: contractor sourcing, procurement, scheduling, quality assurance, and client reporting. We manage the complexity so you don't have to.

### Paving & Landscaping

Driveway paving, garden paths, retaining walls, and landscaping. Quality materials matched to Pretoria's climate and soil conditions, properly installed and finished.

### Civil & Infrastructure

Earthworks, stormwater drainage, retaining structures, and bulk earthwork projects. CIDB-registered and experienced in public- and private-sector contracts.

Who We Are

## Built on Trust. Driven by Quality.

Vuxa Project & Construction is a Pretoria-based contractor with deep roots in the communities we serve across Gauteng. We were founded on a simple principle: every client deserves a build that's done right the first time — no shortcuts, no surprises.

Our team carries hands-on experience across residential, commercial, and civil construction. We hold all required industry registrations and are committed to fair labour practices and meaningful B-BBEE compliance throughout our supply chain.

We serve homeowners, developers, and businesses across Pretoria, Mabopane, Soshanguve, Hammanskraal, and broader Gauteng — with rates that reflect the local market.
